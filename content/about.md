## About

I'm a protein designer at [Science](https://science.xyz/), where I engineer novel proteins for [vision restoration](https://science.xyz/news/new-frontiers-in-optogenetic-research/)
and [biohybrid neural interfaces](https://science.xyz/news/biohybrid-neural-interfaces/).


My work sits at the intersection of synthetic biology and machine learning. I am deeply passionate about using proteins to solve
pressing challenges in human health and environmental sustainability.

Previously, I received a B.S. in chemical engineering and applied math at the University of Nevada, Reno, followed by a PhD at the University of California, Berkeley. Advised by [Jay Keasling](https://keaslinglab.lbl.gov/people/#principal-investigator), I worked at the [Joint Bioenergy Institute](https://www.jbei.org/) engineering polyketide synthases to produce biofuels and bioproducts.
My academic research was generously supported by a [McNair Fellowship](https://mcnairscholars.com/), [NSF GRFP](https://www.nsfgrfp.org/),
and [GEM Fellowship](https://www.gemfellowship.org/).

Outside of the lab, I enjoy hiking with my dog and training
for hybrid strength and running challenges.
